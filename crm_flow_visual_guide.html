Complete Business Flow

1 Get Potential Customer

Someone is interested in your product/service

↓
2 Add as Lead (Admin Portal)

Admin goes to: Leads → New Lead → Fill details → Save

↓
3 Manage in Kanban Board (Admin Portal)

Move lead through stages: New → Contacted → Qualified → Proposal Sent

↓
4 Win the Lead!

Lead accepts your proposal and becomes a customer

↓
5 Convert to Customer (Admin Portal)

Click "Convert to Customer" button → Customer account created

↓
6 Customer Gets Login Access

System sends email with CLIENT portal credentials to customer

↓
7 Create Invoice (Admin Portal)

Sales → Invoices → New Invoice → Select customer → Add items → Send

↓
8 Customer Views & Pays (Client Portal)

Customer logs in /clients → Views invoice → Pays online → ✅ Paid!

↓
9 Deliver Service (Admin Portal)

Create project → Add tasks → Track work → Update customer

↓
10 Happy Customer! 🎉

Project completed → Customer satisfied → Repeat business!

🍕 Simple Restaurant Analogy

👨‍🍳 Kitchen = Admin Portal

  • 🔹 Who: Chefs (Staff/Admins)
  • 🔹 What: Cook food, manage orders
  • 🔹 Access: Full restaurant control
  • 🔹 See: All orders, all customers

YOU NEED: Kitchen key (admin credentials) 🔑

🍽️ Dining Area = Client Portal

  • 🔹 Who: Customers (Clients)
  • 🔹 What: Eat food, pay bill
  • 🔹 Access: Own table only
  • 🔹 See: Own order, own bill

YOU HAVE: Dining seat (client credentials) ✅

💡 Bottom Line: You have a seat in the dining area, but you need access to the kitchen to manage your restaurant!
